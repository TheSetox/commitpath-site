CommitPath privacy

This policy explains how the free, account-free CommitPath app and this website handle information. It applies to CommitPath 1.0 as prepared for Android and iOS release.

Learning information on your device

CommitPath stores personal decks and card references, favorites, bookmarks, reading positions, learning activity, appearance, and advertising preferences in local app storage. The bundled Flashcards and Guided lessons work offline. The current release has no CommitPath account or app-managed sync.

Android app backup is disabled. An iOS device backup may include local app data depending on your settings. Removing the app may remove this data. We cannot recover it from a CommitPath account.

Accounts, purchases, and advertising

The current Release app does not offer sign-in, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. Its app code does not request ads. Development builds may show test ads; they are separate from the Release app described here.

Third-party libraries

The iOS app package includes libraries from Google Mobile Ads, Google User Messaging Platform, and RevenueCat for potential future advertising support. CommitPath 1.0 Release code does not initialize those services or request ads, purchases, or subscriptions. The Android Release package does not include those advertising or RevenueCat libraries. We will review the signed iOS build and update this policy and the store disclosures before enabling those services in a later version. You can read the providers’ Google privacy policy and RevenueCat privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you email support, we and our email provider receive your email address and whatever you include in the message or attachments. We use that information to understand and respond to your request and retain the conversation for follow-up. You can ask us to delete a support conversation by emailing developer@siapknow.com; legal or security obligations may require us to keep some information.

This website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, forms, or browser cookies set by CommitPath. GitHub may process request information, including your IP address, to deliver and protect the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Questions and changes

For privacy questions or requests about information you have sent us, email developer@siapknow.com. We will update this policy before production advertising, accounts, purchases, remote content, or analytics are enabled.

Effective date: 29 September 2026. Visit support.
