Support Privacy

How can we help?

If a card, Guided lesson, personal deck, or part of the Library is not working as expected, send a note. We read support mail at developer@siapknow.com.

Email CommitPath support →

What to include

Tell us what you tried, what happened, and how to repeat it. The topic name, app version, device model, and operating-system version can help. A screenshot is useful if it does not show passwords or other sensitive information.

Offline learning

Flashcards and Guided lessons are bundled with the app and should open without internet. If a topic does not load, tell us its name and the steps that led to the problem.

Your saved learning data

CommitPath has no account or app-managed sync in the current release. Personal decks, favorites, bookmarks, and preferences stay on your device. A device backup may include that data, depending on your platform and backup settings. We cannot retrieve a personal deck from a CommitPath account or restore it remotely.

Have a privacy question? Read the privacy information or email us directly.
